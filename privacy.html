Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 sanjaygl.vercel.app

1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to the personal website and AI Assistant of Sanjay G. L. Your privacy is respected. This Privacy Policy outlines the minimal information collected when you interact with this portfolio, how that information is processed, stored, and protected, and your rights regarding your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

This application collects minimal data necessary to function securely and effectively:

  • Direct Contact Messages: When you submit an inquiry through the contact form, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, subject, and message content.
  • AI Assistant Queries: Questions or prompts submitted to the built-in AI Assistant (via /api/agent or /chat) are processed to provide context-aware answers about Sanjay's portfolio, skills, and background.
  • Technical Diagnostics & Spam Defense: Contact submissions employ honeypot checks and server-side verification to protect against automated spam and unauthorized automated requests.

3. How We Use Information

Collected information is strictly utilized for the following explicit purposes:

  • To respond to direct professional inquiries, project collaborations, and hiring offers.
  • To generate intelligent real-time replies through the portfolio's interactive AI assistant.
  • To prevent automated form abuse and maintain platform reliability.
  • To optimize website navigation and user experience.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption at Rest: When contact submissions are stored in the local development database, sensitive field contents are encrypted using Fernet symmetric key cryptography.
  • HTTPS Transport Security: All data transmitted between your browser and our deployed portfolio is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3).
  • Zero Selling/Monetization: Your personal information is never sold, rented, or commercialized under any circumstances.

5. Third-Party Integrations

This portfolio integrates with select trusted third-party service providers:

  • Vercel: Infrastructure hosting and serverless routing.
  • Large Language Model (LLM) engine powers the AI Assistant responses when enabled.
  • Web3Forms & FormSubmit: Direct email forwarding services used to deliver contact messages to Sanjay's inbox.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not use tracking or marketing cookies. We use standard browser localStorage solely for storing local user interface state:

  • Theme selection (Dark Mode / Light Mode preference).
  • Audio synthesizer settings for interactive sound effects.
  • Temporary AI chat session identifier (non-personally identifiable token).

7. Your Rights & Removal Requests

You have the full right to request access to or deletion of any contact messages you have submitted. If you would like your submitted contact message removed, reach out directly.
